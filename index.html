Security

Lock on launch is always on and cannot be disabled. PIN is exactly 4 characters: letters, digits, or any mix. 5 failed PIN attempts wipe all data. One wrong security-question answer wipes all data. PIN and security question can only be reset through the recovery flow, never edited directly.

Tutorial

Lock screen. Calcsprace always locks on launch. You can't turn this off. Tap the PIN box to bring up your device's keyboard.

PIN rules. Exactly 4 characters. Letters, digits, or any mix. All allowed.

Forgot PIN? Use the security question you set during setup. You get one attempt. A wrong answer wipes everything.

Too many wrong PINs. 5 failed attempts wipe all data. No lockout screen, no countdown. Just a fresh install.

PANIC button. Red button in the top-right of every tab. Wipes everything instantly, no confirmation. Use it if your device is being taken.

Calculator. Standard and scientific. Memory keys on the scientific pad. Live preview shows the result as you type.

Converter. 16 categories. Pick from/to units, enter a value, get the result live.

History. Tap any past result to bring it back into the calculator.

Date calculator. Difference in days between two dates.

Privacy. Everything stays on your device. No sync, no network, no accounts. Wiping is irreversible.
